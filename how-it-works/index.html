How SITE2SCHEDULE AI Works

From field capture to PM validation and government acceptance — an end-to-end audit-backed lifecycle.

01

Project Manager Creates Official Daily Tasks

Project Manager

Sets daily activity targets (e.g. L6-PIP-0245 Welding, Planned 400 joints, High Priority) and assigns them to specific field Site Managers.

02

Site Manager Captures Live Evidence

Site Manager

Supervisors take photos directly using mobile cameras, input reported quantities (e.g. 260 joints), reported progress (65%), and record voice notes.

03

AI Matches Evidence & Detects Objects

Deterministic AI

Simulates high-confidence matching (95%), detects pipe diameters, weld puddles, and verifies that GPS coordinates fall within the corridor.

04

Project Manager Validates & Approves

Project Manager

Reviews side-by-side: original site claim (65%) vs AI suggestion vs engineer measurement. Sets validated progress to 63% and commits to schedule.

05

Government Reviews & Accepts Monitoring State

Government Officer

Reviews incomplete works and validated progress. Approves the reported state without modifying contractual engineering baselines.

Why SITE2SCHEDULE AI Focuses on Level 5 (Pipeline Erection) & Level 6 (Specific Activity)

1. Granular Field Truth

Levels 1–4 are macro planning abstractions. Physical reality happens at L5 (Pipeline Erection), where daily shifts execute L6 specific operations (welding joints, crawler radiography, 3LPE coating, lowering).

2. Automated AI Verification

Computer vision cannot audit an entire project (L1) at once. It inspects geotagged photos against the specific engineering criteria of an L6 activity (e.g. 24" weld root pass, preheating temperature 110°C).

3. Bottom-Up Schedule Rollup

When PMs validate L6 actuals (e.g. 63% vs 65%), the platform mathematically aggregates physical progress into L5 Erection packages, updating L4, L3, L2, and L1 with zero manual data-entry errors.
